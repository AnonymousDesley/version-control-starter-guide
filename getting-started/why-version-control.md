# 🧭 Why Version Control?

Imagine writing a book and saving every draft as "final_v2_revised_REAL_final.docx". That’s chaos.

Version control:
- Tracks every change you make
- Lets you go back in time
- Helps you collaborate safely
- Makes you feel like a time-traveling coder 🕰️

Git is the tool. GitHub is the platform. Together, they’re your creative safety net.
